# Historical PDF archive

This archive makes every preserved historical manuscript PDF available under
its canonical version number. Each filename is the publication identifier.
The current manuscript is [Version 3.29.0](main.pdf).

The sequence `1.0.3`, `2.0.0`, and `3.0.0` is intentional: Versions 2.0.0
and 3.0.0 were major pedagogical and architectural revisions. The later
renumbering follows the MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH policy documented in the README.

matching PDF survives locally; the archive does not invent or relabel an
artifact without source evidence.

Four retained PDFs had obsolete pre-correction title-page labels. They were
rebuilt from their preserved source snapshots and visibly corrected before
archiving: `3.4.6` is published as 3.9.0, `3.4.8` as 3.11.0, `3.8.0` as
3.16.0, and `3.9.0` as 3.17.0. The version-numbering table in the README
records the complete rationale.
